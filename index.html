Karthik had the skills. Meera had the skills and people knew about them. Skills + Visibility + Network = Opportunity.

  • Profile = your shop window. Search ranks complete, keyword-rich profiles higher.
  • Feed = your stage. Early comments and reading time push a post to 2nd and 3rd-degree people.
  • Network = your reach. Every connection widens who can see you.
  • Rewarded: original posts, real conversations, consistency. Punished: engagement bait, spam tags, generic AI text.

MYTHHigh CGPA alone gets you a great job.

CGPA clears the first filter. Companies select on skills, projects and communication. Keep a decent CGPA and build beyond it.

MYTHHard work is enough; good work speaks for itself.

Work speaks only to people who can see it. Karthik's story: talent without visibility is delayed.

MYTHNetworking is only for extroverts.

Online networking is writing, not talking. A thoughtful comment or a short note works for introverts too.

MYTHLinkedIn is for final-years and job seekers.

First-years who start now have three years of posts, connections and growth by placement time.

MYTHSkills matter more than marks, so marks don't matter.

Many companies have CGPA cut-offs. The winning formula: decent CGPA plus skills plus visibility.
